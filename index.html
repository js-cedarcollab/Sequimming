HOW THIS WORKS

You're a Running Start kid trying to get off the Olympic Peninsula — and there's more than one way out. Graduate for real, drop out cool after five shows, become champion of a town, or take a certain classmate's offer. Everything else is fuel for the trip. The 📓 Memory Book keeps score of all four.
• You and your best friend do the summer together — drive to their town first to meet up, then you're a pair
• Anything with a bobbing gold ▾ arrow is playable — walk onto it
• Collect quarters (◉); gas stations turn quarters into gas so you can drive between towns
• Legion Hall's always open for a show; Seattle and the Gorge only pop up for a little while — watch the banner up top
• 📓 Memory Book tracks it all: your four ways off the peninsula, crushes, mixtape, and more
