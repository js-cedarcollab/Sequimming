HOW THIS WORKS

You're a Running Start kid trying to get off the Olympic Peninsula — and there's more than one way out. Graduate for real, drop out cool after five shows, become champion of a town, or take a certain classmate's offer. Everything else is fuel for the trip. The 📓 Memory Book keeps score of all four.
• Walk around, collect quarters (◉) — pizza and records too
• Gas stations turn quarters into gas so you can drive between towns
• Legion Hall's always open for a show; Seattle and the Gorge only show up for a little while, so watch the banner up top when one opens
• The more of a town you explore, the more you become that town — retiree, hippie, or hick. Fill a meter and its Wonder opens up
• 🏛️ badge tracks your tribe; 📓 Memory Book tracks everything you've found
